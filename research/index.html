Research territory

This page makes the research vocabulary behind Todd Harple's public work explicit. The terms are conceptual anchors, not claims that any institution endorses them.

Research questions

  • How does AI change a system when it enters an institution rather than a standalone task?
  • What happens when AI increases the speed or scale of an organization's existing assumptions?
  • How can organizations distinguish data availability from genuine understanding?
  • What role can anthropology, ethnography and social science play in AI strategy?
  • How should human agency be represented in AI-enabled workflows?
  • What can Olympic-scale technology deployments teach us about AI adoption?

Conceptual vocabulary

Human interface with AI The broader social, organizational and cultural interface between people and increasingly capable AI systems—not merely the screen or prompt through which someone interacts with a model.
AI amplification The idea that AI can increase the speed, reach or consistency of an existing system, including systems whose incentives or assumptions are poorly aligned.
Translation Gap The space between technical capability and an organization's ability to translate that capability into coordinated, meaningful outcomes.
Map before prototype A practice of understanding actors, relationships, constraints, incentives and desired outcomes before building an intervention.
Data is not understanding A reminder that measurement and interpretation are related but distinct activities.
Human agency The ability of people and institutions to understand, influence and remain accountable for consequential decisions in AI-enabled systems.
