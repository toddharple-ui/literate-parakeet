Todd Harple, PhD

The Human Interface with AI

Anthropologist. Innovation strategist. AI practitioner. Todd Harple studies what happens when artificial intelligence enters human systems already shaped by behavior, institutions, culture, incentives, history, and meaning.

The interesting question is not only what AI can do. It is what happens when increasingly capable systems enter organizations and cultures that already have assumptions, incentives, narratives, and power structures.

Why this work is grounded in practice

Core propositions

AI amplifies systems

AI does not enter a vacuum. It can accelerate the assumptions, incentives, and misalignments already present in a system.

Data is not understanding

Precision can increase without context increasing. What a system can measure is not necessarily the same as what people need to understand.

Orientation precedes optimization

Before building or optimizing an AI system, understand the people, institutions, relationships, incentives, and outcomes around it.

Human agency is the point

Human-centered AI is not simply about making interfaces pleasant. It is about preserving and extending meaningful human agency.
