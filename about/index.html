About Todd Harple, PhD

Anthropologist • Innovation Strategist • AI Practitioner

Todd Harple works at the intersection of human behavior, emerging technology, innovation, and complex organizational systems. His perspective is grounded in anthropology and nearly two decades of technology and innovation practice at Intel, including work connected to the Olympic Games.

Practice

Intel and Olympic innovation.
Harple's Intel work included Olympic innovation, pathfinding and AI programs across multiple Games cycles. An independent 2024 interview described his role as Intel's Olympics AI Innovation Program Lead and discussed AI applications for athletes, fans and Olympic organizers.
IOC and Olympic AI.
Harple participated in the IOC AI Working Group during the period leading to the Olympic AI Agenda. The IOC launched the Olympic AI Agenda in April 2024 as a strategic initiative for AI across the Olympic Movement.

Questions he explores

  • What changes when AI enters an institution rather than an isolated workflow?
  • How do organizations distinguish information from understanding?
  • When does AI amplify alignment—and when does it amplify misalignment?
  • How can AI extend human agency rather than simply automate decisions?
  • What can anthropology and other social sciences contribute to AI strategy?
